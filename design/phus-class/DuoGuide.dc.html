two displays, one classroom

iPhone Duo

Closed, it is the desk: one task. Open, the board joins as the left page. Half open, it stands on your real desk and he paces on the top half while you work. Nothing resets as the phone moves; everything glides.

The room got wider

New for the inner display: the door with the 11A plate, a notice board, a wall fan that turns, a bookshelf with a globe, a second row of desks, and an Odd Eggs Studio carving. The outer display and every phone keep seeing the middle of the same room.

Applied from the guidelines
1Two levels on the inner display. Closed shows the desk. Open adds the board as a leading pane, the way Mail adds its list.
2Same state everywhere. The task, the timer, the fuse and his mood never restart when the phone opens or closes.
3Controls on the side. Board, Timetable and Logbook down the trailing edge with symbols and titles; Settings at the bottom; the rest in the system overflow menu.
4Nothing in the fold. Half open, both pages step away from the crease and become equal. His pacing stops short of it.
5The room runs full bleed. The classroom extends under the side controls; text and buttons stay inside the safe area.
6Small moves, not rearrangements. Every element glides to its new place. Nothing vanishes and reappears elsewhere.
