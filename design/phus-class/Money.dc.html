Apple’s free trial on the plan picked. The receipt gets a blue “Học thử” stamp and shows the three dates.

4Day five

One note: “Your free week ends Sunday. On Monday, 499.000đ. Settings, two taps, if you’re done.”

5Day seven

The first charge. The receipt is stamped “Paid”. Cancelled? The rooms close quietly and he never mentions it.

Why a trial with a card, done carefully. Trials that take a card convert about twice as well (a median of 37% for 5–9 day trials). This audience forgets, so the receipt shows the charge date before the button, he sends one note two days ahead, and Apple’s refund request is one tap away in Settings for a charge someone forgot. Blinkist’s reminder got 23% more trial starts and 55% fewer complaints.

Học bổng · the scholarship

“Can’t pay right now?” sits under every receipt. One tap, one optional line, a free school year. No proof, no form. Renew it the same way. Structured and Inflow already do this; it costs us only the AI a student uses.

Coming back

Someone whose tuition lapsed gets Apple’s win-back offer once: a free month back. Never a count of how long they were gone.
