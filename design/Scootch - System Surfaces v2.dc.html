Scootch · beyond the app · v2

The monsters get out

Outside the app, every surface is about one monster at a time. A session is a hunt you can watch shrink. Widgets show what's lurking and how long it's been there, so a glance tells you what to do next. Notifications come from the monsters themselves, and one long-press breaks a big monster into small bites. Your Plus finish follows you everywhere. Tap a lurker in the medium widget and the hunt switches to it.

Settings · icon and wallpaper

A new Look group in Settings. Apps can change their own icon, and iOS confirms the change with its own alert. Apps can't change your wallpaper. So Scootch makes the image and you set it: save it to Photos, or add a Shortcuts automation that refreshes it every morning as your world grows. The depth effect over the clock is up to iOS.

08

Built for ADHD brains, using what iOS already has

Five problems, each matched to a native feature. Starting: one press from anywhere. Capture: any app can turn something into a monster. Distraction: a Screen Time shield during a hunt. Time blindness: warnings before the next thing. Memory: the right monster, in the right place, at the right moment. No new habits needed, because it works from places you already touch.

Every way to start · zero to hunting in one moveThe hardest second is the first one. Each of these starts a ten-minute hunt on your oldest lurker, or on whatever you say, without opening the app.
