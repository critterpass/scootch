Gift · how it actually worksApple has no native subscription gifting, so this is the mechanism to confirm with Apple before launch.
1 · Priya buysA non-renewing in-app purchase: Plus gift, 3 months or 1 year. Guideline 3.1.1 allows gifting in-app items; refunds go to the giver.
2 · Scootch mintsA one-time-use subscription offer code, created through the App Store Connect API and sent as a Messages card.
3 · Kofi redeemsIn Apple's redemption sheet. No Scootch account. After the free period it would auto-renew, so he gets a clear reminder.
CutLifetime as a gift. There's no clean way to deliver a non-consumable to someone else.
Open questionUsing offer codes as paid gifts needs Apple's sign-off. Fallback: friend pass seats only.
