The catch · eight, rolled per task

The real task stays the hero. While the timer runs, the headline is always the task itself ("Go email the dentist.") and the trap is a quiet line underneath. When time's up, Scootch asks if you really did it. Only a yes unlocks the catch, and "Not yet" gives you more time. Each new task rolls one of eight catches, seeded by the task so it stays put if you reopen it. Press Play session (ten minutes sped up), then catch him. Sound on.

Made to share

Four moments are worth showing someone: the catch, the monster before you catch it, your world and the week's song. They all open one composer and share one grammar: a mono strip on top, one big word, the real object in the middle, one true sentence and scootch.app at the bottom. Hide the task keeps the numbers and drops the details. Plus frames carry your finish. Try it: switch the moment, the format and the frame, then share.
