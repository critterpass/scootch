How the camera thinksFour modes, one rule: return a single first step small enough to do now. Desk and Room are free; Paper and Screen are Plus, with one free try. Nothing leaves the phone without asking.
DeskFinds objects and picks the one that leaves the fastest.
PaperReads forms and letters, picks the easiest box, translates jargon.
ScreenFinds the one email, tab or message that matters, drafts a first line.
RoomSplits big spaces into zones and hands you the smallest.
Desk and Room are read on this phone with Apple's Vision. Paper and Screen need a bigger model, so the photo is sent encrypted, read, and deleted, and never used for training. The first Paper scan asks first. Before-and-after pictures are saved only if you tap Keep or Share.
