WHO IT'S FOR

For brains where starting is the hard part.

For people with ADHD, and anyone whose to-do list has its own to-do list. Scootch works from places you already touch, so there are no new habits to keep up.

STARTINGOne press, from anywhereAction button, Siri, a widget, Control Center. You're hunting before the doubt kicks in.
CAPTUREOut of your head, fastShare an email or a webpage and it becomes a named monster. Park thoughts by voice mid-hunt.
DISTRACTIONA shield on the feedsDuring a hunt, the apps you choose show Scootch holding the door. Scootch never learns which apps they are.
TIMETime you can seeTime shown as a race, a warning 20 minutes before your next thing, and a soft tap on your wrist.
KINDNESSStarting countsStop at six minutes and the monster still shrinks. No streaks to break, ever.
Scootch is a support tool for starting things. It doesn't diagnose, treat or cure ADHD or anything else.
