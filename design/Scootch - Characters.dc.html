Monster zoo

Every task hatches into a monster picked from its meaning, then varied by ink, size, eyes, mouth and accessories.

Monster zooTwenty body types picked from the task: a tooth for the dentist, a receipt hydra for taxes, a sock for laundry, a beetle for bugs. On top of that, each monster gets its own ink, size, eyes (one to three, mismatched or on stalks), mouth, horns, antennae and legs. No two tasks hatch the same.
