# Undesigned states

States the app shows that no design board draws. Each was built from existing
components and tokens. The founder reviews this list; a row leaves it when the
state is designed or removed. Scootch's list is in the history of this file.

| Screen | State | Reason |

| Classroom, your desk | The gold star sticker left by a rare finish | The brief names it; no board draws it. The notice board's gold star, a third of its size, with a darker edge |

| Board | His swap line | The brief gives a swap one line; no board shows where. It takes the remark's place at the foot of the board |
| Board | A task's menu (tap or long-press) | Every gesture needs a tap alternative and reword has no swipe. A chalk-green panel of the board's own pills: onto the desk, reword, tick off, erase |
