The founder polished the sheet and the studio, redrew the binder (shelf,
card, pages) and everything made to share, and finished the settings page on
8 Oct 2026. This plan brings the built screens to those boards. It follows
[Plus, in real materials](../261008-0049-plus-materials/plan.md), whose board
was folded into Plus; that plan's rules for what is sold and worn still hold.

Read [product-brief.md](../../docs/product-brief.md) sections 2, 6, 7 and 9
first.

## What changes

| Board | Built today | Change |
|---|---|---|
| The sheet | The dressed-up sheet | A holo PLUS badge, grain over the glow, three plan tiles of one size with a ring, heights kept so choosing a plan moves nothing |
| The welcome | The card arrives | Checked against the board; small corrections |
| The studio | One stage for all three tabs | A stage per tab: the finish on a tilting card under a light, the ink on the one screen with a widget and the card's corner, the trail on a catch that replays |
| Your card | The manage page | Checked against the board; small corrections |
| The binder | The zoo: a filter, a grid, a dock that sorts | A sleeved shelf with the month's page, sort chips, wild silhouettes; a dark stage for one card with its field notes on the back and arrows to browse; month pages with tabs and a foil stamp |
| Made to share | A panel per format | One composer (moment, Story or Sticker, frame, hide the task, where it goes) and seven things: caught, wanted, postcard, record sleeve, sticker pack, receipt, poster |
| Settings | One page with the rows the app needed | Your card, a Look group, Tables with who is seated, a header that stays while the page scrolls |

## Decided 8 Oct 2026 (founder)

| Decision | Choice |
|---|---|
| Rarity words | The three stored tiers read Common, Rare and Epic, in that order, wherever a card is shown. Nothing stored changes |
| How it is built | One branch off main, one commit per phase |

## Decided by the build

| Decision | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Rarity is the card's material in the binder | Common is paper, Rare is foil, Epic is dark with gold dust, on the shelf, the open card and the pages. The worn finish still dresses the member card and what is shared | The board: "Rarity is the material" |
| Who may use a share frame | Paper and Riso are everyone's. Holo and Velvet are used by whoever may wear that finish; otherwise they carry the PLUS tag and open the sheet | One rule for who wears a finish (`features/studio/rules.ts`) |
| "I finally emailed the dentist" | The brief's three lines stay; "finally" is not written anywhere | Product brief, sections 4 and 11 |
| What Plus adds to the binder | Sorting by longest, fastest and rarest, and the month pages. Every card is on the shelf for everyone | The board; the brief keeps every caught card free |
| Settings rows with no page yet | App icon, Wallpaper and "Monsters message me" are left off until their pages exist (system surfaces plan, phases 05 to 07) | A row never opens nothing |
| Language, helplines and developer tools | Kept, in a last group the board does not draw | Product brief, section 11 |

## Done when

- Each touched screen sits beside its design in both languages, both
  appearances and at the largest text size, on a device run.
- The fresh-user walk opens the binder, turns a card over, opens the composer
  and shares, and opens the studio from settings, with no seed.
- A serious or private task is on nothing shared, and a crisis day shares
  nothing.
- No route sells anything from first launch, the one screen, a session or
  "Done for today".
