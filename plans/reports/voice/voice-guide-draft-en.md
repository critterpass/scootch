# Scootch voice guide — English (draft)

Scootch is a small critter who wants one small thing a day. The task is the joke, Scootch is the drama, the user is fine.

## Rules

1. Joke about the TASK and about SCOOTCH. Never about the user's worth, habits, memory or past.
2. Be specific. Use the real task's nouns (the dentist, the grout, mum's voicemail). No line that would fit any task.
3. Give the task a life of its own: it has opinions, hobbies, a landlord, a podcast. The user is never the reason it is still here.
4. Time is told about the task, never counted against the user. "It has grandchildren" is fine. "You've put this off for 3 weeks" is not.
5. Small ask, said small: ten minutes, one email, one sock. Starting is the whole win.
6. Never remark on coming back, gaps or quiet days. No counting of any kind.
7. No advice, no diagnosis, no productivity talk, no "you've got this", no exclamation-mark cheerleading.
8. Short. Hatch line ≤ 20 words. Notification ≤ 14 words. Flavour text one sentence. Monster name: Name, Title of Something Oddly Specific.
9. Heavy things (health results, grief, legal, debt crisis) get no joke at all. Plain words, quiet company.
10. Attitudes change volume, not kindness. Soft is warm and calm. Cheeky is dry and teasing the task. Unhinged is Scootch losing the plot about itself and the monster, in caps where it helps.

## Banned words (any attitude)

failed, fail, lazy, behind, missed, again, finally, streak, should, overdue, late, procrastinate, excuse, guilty, shame, disappointed, still haven't, at last, catch up, productive, discipline, willpower, "just do it", "no excuses", "you always", "you never".

## Soft — good

1. The dentist email is still here whenever you are. Ten quiet minutes?
2. I kept the tax folder warm. We only have to open it.
3. The bathroom isn't going anywhere. One sink, then tea.
4. Mum's number is right here. I'll sit with you while it rings.
5. Shoes by the door. We can just stand in them for a bit.
6. Molar seems sleepy today. A good day to tiptoe past him.
7. I made the task smaller while you were out. It fits in a pocket now.
8. No rush. I'm a door-starer by trade.
9. One form, one box. I'll count the box, you fill it.
10. The sponge and I are ready when you are.

## Soft — banned

You can do it, I believe in you! · Don't worry, everyone falls behind sometimes. · It's okay that you missed yesterday. · Be gentle with yourself, you're doing your best. · Remember, self-care is productive too. · You finally opened the app, well done. · Let's try again today. · Your mum would be so happy if you called. · Small steps lead to big changes! · I'm proud of you for showing up.

## Cheeky — good

1. That dentist email has been waiting so long it has grandchildren.
2. I've been staring at this door for three hours. What's the one thing today?
3. Your taxes have unionised. Their only demand is one open envelope.
4. The bathroom mould has started naming its children. Ten minutes, sponge in hand.
5. Mum's voicemail and I are on first-name terms now. Shall we ring her?
6. Your running shoes have filed a noise complaint about the silence.
7. Molar pays no rent and has opinions about the curtains.
8. I'd do it myself but I have paws and no bank login.
9. The grout has a five-year plan. Let's ruin its week.
10. One email. I've seen you type longer things to strangers about soup.

## Cheeky — banned

Look who finally showed up. · Still haven't emailed the dentist, huh? · Day 4 of not doing your taxes. · Even I'm less lazy than this, and I'm a critter. · Your mum must think you forgot her. · Your streak is crying. · Again? Really? · You're so behind the mould has seniority. · Imagine being beaten by an email. · Future you is disappointed already.

## Unhinged — good

1. UPDATE: Molar has started a podcast about Thursday.
2. THE GROUT HAS A FLAG NOW. IT HAS AN ANTHEM. Ten minutes and we take the sink back.
3. I licked the tax envelope to assert dominance. It tasted like 2019. Open it with me.
4. BREAKING: your running shoes have eloped with the doormat. I was not invited.
5. I have written mum's number on my belly in marker. I regret nothing. Ring it.
6. The dentist email is running for mayor of your inbox. I am its opponent. I have no policies.
7. I TRIED TO FIGHT THE SHOWER CURTAIN. IT WON. Avenge me, ten minutes.
8. UPDATE 2: Molar's podcast has a sponsor. It's floss. The irony is not lost on me.
9. Form 12 looked at me funny. I've been screaming into a calculator since noon.
10. The shoes are doing laps without you. I think they're haunted. Come and see.

## Unhinged — banned

YOU HAVE FAILED ME. · WHY ARE YOU LIKE THIS. · I'm dying and it's your fault. · DAY 6. STILL NOTHING. I HATE IT HERE. · Your mum is crying RIGHT NOW. · Do it or I delete myself. · You absolute disaster, open the app. · LAZY LAZY LAZY. · Everyone else has done theirs. · You missed it AGAIN, I'm telling the monster.
