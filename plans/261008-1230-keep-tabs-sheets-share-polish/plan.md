## What the founder saw

| Where | What was wrong | Change |
|---|---|---|
| The Plus sheet | The member card lay over the title and the close control | A sheet that is a whole page is a page sheet with ordinary layout; nothing on it is laid out by the sheet's own scroll correction |
| "Haunt a friend" under a set task | A full row, louder than the task | The small glass chip, centred, as before |
| The haunt sheet | A full-height sheet, blank with no friend | A sheet as tall as what is on it, its two choices at the foot, and with no friend yet a short guide to what a haunt is |
| Settings | Blank card pictures, no picture on Wallpaper, words that start on different lines | Every Look row has its picture, on one grid |
| Your card without Plus | A bare panel and three rows | Scootch beside what free is, the card that Plus is, and rows with their pictures |
| A locked Plus thing | A tap did nothing in the composer and the studio had no way to Plus | A tap opens the sheet, wherever the screen it is on may sell |
| The world, the binder and the record | Three screens joined by two buttons | One place with three tabs at the foot and a movement between them |
| The share screens | A different layout on each, controls floating under the picture, no way in or out but a cut | One composer, opened as a sheet: the picture fills what room there is, the choices sit under it, one dock at the foot |
| The card on its dark stage | Four loose controls | One dock |
| The postcard's postmark | A missing glyph and a month cut short | A dot, and the month as the card writes dates |

## Decided by the build

| Decision | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The tabs are not swiped | A tap on the tab only | The pages beside home are swiped; a second sideways swipe inside one of them would fight it (founder) |
| World to binder | The island pulls back and fades while the shelf comes forward from larger | "Zoom out the island, the monsters fade and the card grid appears" (founder) |
| World to record | The island's sand tips up into the record, which turns once as it lands; the arm comes last | The island is a disc seen from the side and the record is one seen from above |
| Binder to record | The shelf steps back, the record drops in turning | No shared shape; kept short |
| One header | Each tab has the same title and line under it; the close control stays put | Three screens are one place |
| What the docks held | "Share this page", "Share this week" and "Keep this record" are glass chips in their tab | The foot is the tabs' |
| A locked frame in the composer | Opens the sheet when the composer was opened from the binder, the world or the record; rests when it was opened from the reveal | Nothing is sold on the reveal (house rules) |
| Where a sheet's top is | A sheet never keeps clear of the status bar: it is already under it | The window's inset was applied inside the sheet |
